# LB's: product

LB's Hemp Cafe & Lounge, Salt Lake, Kolkata. One codebase serves two very different jobs.

## The guest site (persuade, then order)

People at a table (after scanning the QR code) or at home. They browse the menu, order to their table or for
takeaway, book a table, follow their order live, play games while they wait and collect stamps on LB's card.
Mostly phones, often one-handed, sometimes in in-app browsers (Instagram, WhatsApp, the Google app).

## The admin (operate)

Staff on their own phones during service, owners and managers sometimes on a laptop.

- **Servers** take walk-in orders, settle bills, answer table calls, handle bookings and mark dishes sold out.
- **Chefs** see what to make and move orders to preparing and ready.
- **Managers and owners** also edit the menu and prices, run offers, read reports and change settings.

What matters: the next action is obvious and one tap away, nothing important is hidden behind scrolling or tiny
text, and it works one-handed under pressure on a busy night. Alerts (new order, a table calling, an order ready)
must be impossible to miss. Laptops get the same screens with more room.

Constraints: Indian rupees, GST 18% split CGST/SGST, Asia/Kolkata time, plain sentence-case copy.
